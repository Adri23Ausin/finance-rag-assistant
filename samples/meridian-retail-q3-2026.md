# Meridian Retail — Third quarter 2026 results

*Fictional company and figures, for demonstrating the assistant.*

## Highlights

- Revenue grew 5.2% to EUR 2,318 million (Q3 2025: EUR 2,204 million); like-for-like sales grew 3.9%.
- Online sales reached 27% of revenue, up from 24% a year earlier.
- Gross margin was 41.8%, 60 basis points lower than in Q3 2025, because of promotional activity in September.
- EBITDA rose 3.1% to EUR 412 million; the EBITDA margin was 17.8%.
- Net profit was EUR 186 million, up 2.2%.
- Net debt fell to EUR 640 million, 0.4 times trailing EBITDA.

## Outlook

Meridian keeps its full-year guidance of revenue growth between 4% and 6% and expects the gross margin to recover to around 42.5% in the fourth quarter as the promotional season ends. The company will open 35 stores in 2026, mainly in Poland and Romania, and close 12 smaller stores in Spain.

## Dividend

The Board confirmed an interim dividend of EUR 0.15 per share, payable on 2 December 2026, unchanged from last year.
